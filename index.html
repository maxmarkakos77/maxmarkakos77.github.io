Player Bio

Built for the
Next Level

Max Markakos is an elite 2009 birth-year center who brings an uncommon combination of size, hockey IQ, and compete level to every game and practice. At 6'3" and 198 lbs, he plays a physically dominant game while maintaining the vision and playmaking ability of a true two-way center.

Currently competing at the 18U AAA level with the Washington Little Caps — one of the premier programs in the Mid-Atlantic — Max has earned a reputation as a dominant faceoff specialist with a 71% win rate in competitive play. His combination of body positioning, anticipation, and raw strength makes him college-ready.

Max sharpens his game with access to NHL Players and Washington Capitals training staff, giving him a professional development edge rare at his age. A student and multi-sport athlete at Landon School in Bethesda, MD, he brings academic discipline and genuine character to everything he does.
